Decision-Centric Triage (Cognitive Load Reduced)

InboxIQ reduces cognitive load by filtering, prioritising, and triaging incoming work so your team focuses on what matters.

It unifies Voice IVR transcripts, Social, Email, Forms, Chat, and CRM into one decision stream, then routes, escalates, or auto-handles based on urgency and impact — before humans touch the queue.

  • One unified intake stream.
  • Clear, auditable decisions.
