One Core Feature
AI-Powered Triage + Automation (Cognitive Load Reduced)

InboxIQ uses AI to automatically classify, prioritize, and route incoming work—so your team focuses on solving, not sorting.

It unifies Voice IVR transcripts, Social, Email, Forms, Chat, and CRM into one decision stream, then routes, escalates, or auto-handles based on urgency and impact — before humans touch the queue.

  • One unified intake stream.
  • Clear, auditable decisions.
  • Less noise, more focus.

How it works in 3 steps

  1. 1.
    Connect unified intake sources

    Bring Voice IVR, Social, Email, Forms, Chat, and CRM into one intake stream. No complex workflow setup required.

  2. 2.
    Decision engine filters and prioritises

    InboxIQ detects intent, urgency, and risk, then decides what needs action, who owns it, and what can be auto-handled.

  3. 3.
    Teams focus on what matters

    Actionable decisions land with owners and SLAs, while noise is filtered out — reducing decision fatigue and keeping queues calm.
