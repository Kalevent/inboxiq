Six reasons founders stay in Gmail instead of hiring support

1. Stop composing the same reply for the 50th time

InboxIQ classifies every inbound email and places a draft in the thread — grounded in your knowledge base and past replies — before you even open it.

2. Handle volume growth without a support hire

Growing from 200 to 500 customers doubles email load. InboxIQ handles the increase without adding headcount — at 3% the cost of a junior hire.

3. Customers get a reply at 11pm on a Friday

A draft appears the moment an email arrives — any time of day. High-confidence replies can send automatically; everything else waits for your approval.

4. Connect support@, sales@, and your inbox — together

One account connects multiple inboxes. Labels and drafts appear inside each person's Gmail independently. No password sharing, no shared login.

5. AI detects meeting requests and includes your availability

When an email asks for a demo or call, the draft reply includes available slots pulled from Google or Outlook Calendar. No Calendly link. No back-and-forth.

6. See what customers are actually asking about

InboxIQ surfaces top contact topics — this week, this month, trending up or down. One click opens Automation Studio pre-filled with the topic as a trigger.
