InboxIQ works inside Gmail — not in a separate dashboard. Once you connect your inbox, the AI classifies incoming emails, applies a label, and places a draft reply in the thread. You open the email, review the draft, and send.

Connect your inbox now and we will send two test emails to it straight away — one support query and one meeting request — so you can see exactly what InboxIQ does before any real customer email arrives.

What happens in the next 5 minutes

  1. You connect Gmail or Outlook — takes 30 seconds
  2. InboxIQ sends two demo emails to your inbox
  3. The AI classifies them and creates draft replies
  4. You open Gmail and see labels and drafts already in the threads

One of the demo emails is a meeting request. If you connect your Google or Outlook Calendar in Settings → Integrations, the draft reply for that email will include your real available time slots — no Calendly link, no back-and-forth.
