            Period Channel Amount (GBP) Notes
            No spend entries yet.

            MRR = sum of active (paid, non-trialing) subscription plan prices.

            Churn % = subscriptions canceled this calendar month ÷ (active + canceled this month). Uses Subscription.updated_at as cancel proxy.

            LTV = ARPU ÷ monthly churn rate (average customer lifetime × revenue). Shows "—" until churn data exists.

            CAC = total spend this month ÷ new paid customers this month. Enter spend above to unlock.

            LTV:CAC ≥ 3 is the healthy SaaS benchmark. AI SaaS: adjust LTV for per-customer compute cost.
