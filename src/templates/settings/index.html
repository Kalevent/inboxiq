    Draft Reply Generation

    Automatically generate AI-powered draft replies using your knowledge base and conversation history. Drafts appear on eligible tickets and require human review before sending.

    {% if not draft_reply_has_access %}
    Draft reply feature requires Business plan or active trial. Contact sales to upgrade.
    {% endif %}
    How Draft Replies Work
    • 1. When a ticket is triaged, the AI analyzes the customer's message and relevant context.
    • 2. If enabled, it searches your knowledge base for relevant documentation and past interactions.
    • 3. A draft reply is generated and attached to the ticket for your review.
    • 4. You can edit, approve, or discard the draft before sending to the customer.

    Tip: Upload knowledge base articles in Settings → Integrations → Knowledge Base to improve draft quality.
