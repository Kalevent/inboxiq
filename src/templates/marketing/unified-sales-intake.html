Why Manual Sales Intake Breaks

Lead response SLAs slip

Reps see high-intent leads after delays because everything looks the same in the inbox or CRM queue.

Spam overwhelms routing

Bots and noise drain SDR attention, hiding buyers ready to talk.

Ownership gets fuzzy

Duplicated forms create conflicting assignments with no audit trail.

What Is Unified Sales Intake?

Intent detection before CRM

Classify leads by persona, product fit, and urgency before they ever create a record.

Spam and noise suppression

Filter bots, low-intent spam, and duplicates automatically.

Instant routing with audit

Assign owners and queues with a clear rationale and log every decision.

How Unified Sales Intake Works

Connect forms, chat, and inboxes

Route website forms, chat transcripts, and inbound sales@ into one intake layer.

Define intent and urgency rules

Score for product fit, buying signals, and timing. Tag spam on arrival.

Assign and notify instantly

Hot leads route to owners with SLA timers; low-intent records stay suppressed.

Result: faster speed-to-lead, cleaner CRM data, and fewer wasted touches.

Auditable routing
