Critical account events ignored

A CRM notification about an enterprise account going dormant or a deal going cold arrives as an email — and sits unread in a shared inbox.

No urgency classification

{# ── How It Works ──────────────────────────────────────────── #}
How It Works

CRM event to routed, owned decision

1

Route CRM notifications to InboxIQ

Configure your CRM to send notification emails to your connected InboxIQ inbox — or push events directly via the Intake API for programmatic integration.

2

AI reads the event and scores urgency

InboxIQ classifies the event type (churn risk, payment failure, deal update, renewal reminder) and scores urgency. High-risk events are escalated immediately.

3

Routed to the account owner

Each CRM event is assigned to the correct account manager or team based on your routing rules. The account owner gets the event with urgency context — no digging through CRM required.
