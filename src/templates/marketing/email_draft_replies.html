{# ── The core problem ──────────────────────────────────────── #}

The problem with repetitive support replies

If 40% of your support emails ask the same 20 questions, you're writing the same answers over and over — or copying from a doc you have to hunt down first. Every reply takes 3–7 minutes. Across 50 emails a week, that's 3–6 hours of your time on answers you've written before.

  • › Opening your KB to find the right article
  • › Rewording the same answer in a friendly tone
  • › Inconsistency when different teammates answer the same question differently
  • › No record of which KB article was used in the reply

What InboxIQ does instead

After triage classifies an email as support or billing, InboxIQ automatically searches your knowledge base using the email's intent and key phrases, assembles a draft reply from the most relevant article, and presents it in your dashboard — ready to review in seconds.

  • ✓ Draft ready before you open the email
  • ✓ Grounded in your actual documentation — not hallucinated
  • ✓ Source KB article cited so you can verify the answer
  • ✓ You review, edit if needed, then send from your own inbox

Connects to the knowledge base you already have

InboxIQ doesn't require you to build a new knowledge base. It connects to the documentation you've already written — wherever it lives. The richer your KB, the better the drafts.

  • ✓ Notion pages and databases
  • ✓ Confluence spaces
  • ✓ HelpScout Docs
  • ✓ Intercom Articles
  • ✓ Plain-text or Markdown upload

No KB yet? Start with a plain-text doc covering your 10 most common questions. InboxIQ will tell you which questions have no matching article so you know what to write next.

KB gap detection — a side benefit you'll notice quickly

When InboxIQ can't find a matching KB article for an inbound question, it flags the gap in your dashboard. After a few weeks you'll have a clear list of the questions your documentation doesn't answer — and exactly how many times each question was asked.

Most teams find 5–10 high-frequency questions with no article in the first month. Writing those articles improves every future draft and reduces support volume.

{# ── Human control guarantee ────────────────────────────────── #}

You are always in control. InboxIQ never sends on your behalf.

Every draft is a suggestion. It sits in your InboxIQ dashboard until you act on it. You can review, edit, discard, or send — but the decision is always yours. InboxIQ has no write access to your inbox; it cannot send, delete, or move email.

Review

{# ── Why grounded matters ──────────────────────────────────── #}

Why grounded retrieval matters more than raw AI generation

A generic AI assistant might generate a plausible-sounding answer that contradicts your actual policy or pricing. InboxIQ's drafts are retrieval-augmented — the model can only use content from your KB. If the answer isn't in your documentation, InboxIQ tells you rather than making something up.

Generic AI Generates a confident-sounding answer that may be wrong, outdated, or inconsistent with your actual policies
InboxIQ Retrieves the relevant section of your KB, grounds the draft in it, and cites the source — so you can verify in 5 seconds

Feedback loop — drafts get better every week

When you accept a draft unchanged, InboxIQ records that as a positive signal. When you edit before sending, it learns what you changed and why. When you discard, it flags the draft for review.

Over 4–6 weeks, draft acceptance rates typically rise from ~60% to ~85% as the model learns your tone, common exceptions, and the edge cases your KB doesn't fully cover yet.

Draft acceptance rate is tracked in your dashboard — a clear signal of how well InboxIQ is calibrated to your style.
