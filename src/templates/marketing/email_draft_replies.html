{# ── The problem / solution ────────────────────────────────── #}

The problem: you're writing the same replies over and over

If 40% of your support emails ask the same questions, every reply still takes 3–7 minutes — hunting down the right KB article, rewording it in a friendly tone, then formatting and sending. At 50 emails/week that's 3–6 hours on answers you've already written before.

  • › Hunting your KB every time for the same article
  • › Inconsistent answers when teammates handle the same question differently
  • › No record of which KB article backed your reply
  • › Context switching between inbox, KB, and reply window

The fix: draft ready before you open the email

InboxIQ classifies the email, retrieves the most relevant KB article using semantic search, assembles a grounded reply, and places it in your dashboard. You open the email — the answer is already there, cited from your own documentation.

  • ✓ Draft appears in dashboard before you open the email
  • ✓ Grounded in your KB — not hallucinated
  • ✓ Source article cited so you verify in 5 seconds
  • ✓ You review, edit if needed, send from your own inbox

New • AI Meeting Scheduling

Meeting requests answered with available times — automatically

When an email asks to schedule a call, demo, or appointment, InboxIQ detects the intent and fetches available slots directly from your connected calendar. The draft reply already includes times for the recipient to choose from — no Calendly link, no back-and-forth.

  • ✓ Detects meeting requests automatically — "can we book a call?", "when are you free?"
  • ✓ Pulls real availability from Google Calendar or Outlook Calendar
  • ✓ Slots included inline in the draft — recipient just replies with their choice
  • ✓ You review and send — same approval flow as every other draft

Connect your calendar under Settings → Integrations. Supports Google Calendar and Microsoft Outlook Calendar.
