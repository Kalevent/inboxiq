{# ── How It Works ──────────────────────────────────────────── #}
How It Works

Social signal to routed, owned decision

1

Route social notifications to InboxIQ

Configure your social platform to forward DM notifications and escalation alerts to your InboxIQ inbox — or push events directly via the Intake API for programmatic integration.

2

AI reads the signal and scores urgency

InboxIQ classifies the signal type (DM complaint, escalation, partnership enquiry, brand mention) and scores urgency. High-risk signals from existing customers or high-profile accounts are escalated immediately.

3

Routed to the right owner

Customer DMs go to the account owner. PR escalations go to comms. Partnership enquiries go to BD. Each signal follows your routing rules — automatically, with urgency context attached.
