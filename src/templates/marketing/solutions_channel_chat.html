{# ── How It Works ──────────────────────────────────────────── #}
How It Works

Chat handoff to routed, owned decision

1

Chat platforms forward handoffs to InboxIQ

Intercom, Drift, Zendesk Chat, and most live chat tools can send handoff notifications to an email address or webhook. InboxIQ connects to that destination and picks up every handoff automatically.

2

AI reads the transcript and scores urgency

InboxIQ reads the chat context, detects sentiment, identifies the customer tier, and assigns an urgency score. Churn risk, billing issues, and enterprise escalations are surfaced immediately.

3

Routed to the right owner with full context

The handoff lands with a named owner, urgency context, and suggested next step — not a wall of text. Your team acts, not sorts.
