Layer 1 • AI Triage

The decision engine that runs before you open your inbox

Every email is processed before you see it — classified by intent, urgency detected, ownership assigned. No rules to write. The model learns your patterns and improves as you correct it.

  • ✓Intent classification — support, billing, sales, spam, transactional, and custom categories
  • ✓Urgency detection — surface escalations before they become fires
  • ✓Sentiment analysis — flag frustrated customers automatically
  • ✓Human override — one click to correct; model learns immediately

Triage runs on every inbox independently. No shared data between accounts.

1.5–2 hours recovered per person, per day.

{# ── Layer 2: Draft replies ─────────────────────────────────── #}

How KB-grounded drafts work

  1. 1.InboxIQ reads the email and detects the question or request.
  2. 2.It searches your connected knowledge base for relevant articles.
  3. 3.A draft reply is generated, grounded in your actual documentation.
  4. 4.The draft appears in your dashboard — review, edit, or discard.
  5. 5.You send from your own inbox. InboxIQ never sends on your behalf.
Supported: Notion, Confluence, HelpScout Docs, Intercom Articles, plain-text upload.
Layer 2 • Draft Replies

Drafts grounded in your knowledge base. You stay in control.

InboxIQ never sends email on your behalf. It prepares a reply grounded in your actual documentation so you spend 30 seconds reviewing instead of 5 minutes writing from scratch.

  • ✓Drafts appear in the dashboard — never sent without your review
  • ✓KB gap detection — repeated questions with no article trigger a notification
  • ✓AI meeting scheduling — detects scheduling requests and includes available calendar slots in the draft. No Calendly required.
{# ── Scheduling ────────────────────────────────────────────── #}
AI Scheduling • New

Meeting requested? A booking link is already in the draft.

When InboxIQ detects a meeting request, it generates a scheduling link and embeds it in the draft reply automatically. No Calendly. No back-and-forth.

  1. 1.AI detects "can we find a time?" in the incoming email.
  2. 2.A signed booking link is embedded in the draft reply — one click for the visitor.
  3. 3.Visitor picks a slot. Calendar event and Meet/Teams link sent to both parties.
  • ✓Works with Google Calendar and Outlook Calendar
  • ✓No third-party scheduling tool required

{# ── Layer 3: Automation Studio ─────────────────────────────── #}
Layer 3 • Automation Studio

Rules that fire on what the AI decided — not just keywords

Traditional email rules match on sender or subject line. InboxIQ rules trigger on triage output: intent, urgency, sentiment, and category. Write rules in plain English.

  • ✓Natural-language rule builder — "If support and urgent, notify Slack #oncall"
  • ✓Actions — label, archive, forward, assign, webhook, draft, reply with template
  • ✓Audit trail — every execution logged with the AI decision that triggered it

WHEN support and urgent  THEN notify Slack #oncall

WHEN sentiment is frustrated  THEN flag for founder review

WHEN transactional  THEN archive silently
