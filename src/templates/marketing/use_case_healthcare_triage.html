{# ── Clinical Requests & Approvals ───────────────────────── #}
Requests & Approvals in Clinical Operations

Prior authorisation, referrals, and access requests — handled from the inbox

Prior authorisation as an approval workflow

  • PA requests arrive by email from referring physicians and payers — InboxIQ classifies and routes to the correct clinical reviewer

  • Reviewer responds from their existing inbox — decision is logged with timestamp and clinical classification

  • SLA escalation triggers if no response within the configured window — default 24 hours for urgent PA requests (Business plan)

  • Audit trail supports CMS and HIPAA compliance documentation requirements

Staff access requests and referral intake

  • System access requests for clinical staff are classified as IT/admin requests and routed to the appropriate approver

  • Referral intake emails are classified by specialty and urgency, routed to the relevant department

  • All decision records are stored with classification basis for compliance audit — no portal login required for referring physicians

Data & AI privacy

  • BYOL — your AI key, your data boundary

    Connect your own OpenAI or Azure OpenAI key. Patient data goes directly to your provider — InboxIQ never processes or stores the payload.

  • Zero training on PHI

    OpenAI ZDR policy is enabled by default. Your referral content is never used to train models.

  • Self-hosted option (roadmap)

    For NHS trusts and regulated providers who need on-premise deployment — on the roadmap for 2025.

Regulatory alignment

  • HIPAA access logging

    Every email access event is logged with user identity, timestamp, and action — satisfying HIPAA §164.312(b) audit controls.

  • UK CQC inspection readiness

    Exportable decision logs support CQC evidence requirements for clinical governance and referral pathway management.

  • GDPR data minimisation

    Role-scoped access means only the assigned clinician or coordinator can see a given referral — not the whole team inbox.
