What Triage Intelligence classifies

Four dimensions resolved before your team reads it

Intent

Support, billing, feature request, feedback, or transactional — classified from the full email body, not just subject line keywords.

Urgency

P1 through P4 scoring based on tone, context, and churn signals — not just the word "urgent" in the subject.

Owner

The right agent, team, or department assigned automatically based on intent, urgency, and your routing rules.

Rationale

Why was this classified P1? Why was it routed to support and not billing? Every decision is explained and logged.
