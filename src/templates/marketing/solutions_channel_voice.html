{# ── How It Works ──────────────────────────────────────────── #}
How It Works

IVR transcript to routed, owned decision

1

IVR platforms deliver transcripts to InboxIQ

Most IVR and call recording platforms (Twilio, Aircall, RingCentral) can deliver transcripts to an email address or webhook endpoint. InboxIQ connects to that destination and processes every transcript automatically.

2

AI reads the transcript and scores urgency

InboxIQ classifies the call type (support complaint, billing dispute, sales enquiry, churn risk) and scores urgency. High-priority calls from existing customers are escalated immediately — not buried in a queue.

3

Routed to the right owner with context

The follow-up lands with a named owner, urgency score, and a plain-English summary of the call — not a wall of transcript text. Your team acts, not reads.
