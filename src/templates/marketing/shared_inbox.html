Why Shared Inboxes Get Messy

Shared mailboxes (support@, sales@, hello@) break down when multiple people work from the same inbox. The result is predictable:

  • Two people reply to the same email
  • Important requests get buried
  • Ownership is unclear (“Who is handling this?”)
  • Follow-ups slip through the cracks
  • No consistent triage or prioritisation process

InboxIQ fixes this at the intake step: AI email triage plus routing and ownership—built for teams.

Shared Inbox Features Powered by AI

Shared Inbox FAQ

What is a shared inbox?

A shared inbox is a mailbox used by multiple people (e.g., support@). InboxIQ adds AI triage, ownership, and routing to keep it organised.

How does InboxIQ prevent duplicate replies?

By assigning ownership, routing to queues, and making status clear—so the team knows what’s handled and what’s pending.

Can I use InboxIQ without changing my whole stack?

Yes. Start with triage and routing. You can integrate deeper over time with workflows and webhook intake.

Does it work with other channels?

Yes. The same intake brain can process forms, chat transcripts, CRM events, and internal tools via webhooks.
