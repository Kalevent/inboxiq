Why not a shared mailbox?

Shared mailboxes (support@, hello@) work until they don't. When two people work from the same inbox, emails get missed, double-replied, or sit unowned. InboxIQ's approach is different: AI triage runs on each person's own inbox.

Shared mailbox

  • ✗ Double replies happen
  • ✗ No clear ownership
  • ✗ Everything visible to all
  • ✗ One inbox, all chaos

InboxIQ

  • ✓ Each inbox stays private
  • ✓ AI decides who acts
  • ✓ Same triage logic per inbox
  • ✓ No migration, ever
InboxIQ never stores email body content. Labels and triage decisions are written back to each person's inbox. Data stays with your provider.
