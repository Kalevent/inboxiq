{# ── What counts as an AI decision ─────────────────────────── #}

What counts as an AI decision?

One AI decision = one email processed through the triage pipeline (classify, prioritise, detect urgency, assign). Draft reply generation uses a separate allocation from the chat quota. Automation rule executions are not AI decisions — only the triage step that triggered them counts.

50 emails/week

≈ 200 AI decisions/mo — well within Pro

150 emails/week

≈ 650 AI decisions/mo — comfortably on Pro

300 emails/week

≈ 1,300 AI decisions/mo — Business recommended

{# ── ROI calculation ───────────────────────────────────────── #}

What does InboxIQ actually cost to run?

The AI model cost for triage is roughly £1–£2/month per inbox at typical B2B SaaS email volumes (300 emails/week). The rest of your subscription covers infrastructure, the dashboard, automation engine, and KB retrieval.

Compare that to the cost of a person spending 90 minutes per day sorting email: at a blended cost of £30/hr, that's £675/month in lost productivity per inbox.
