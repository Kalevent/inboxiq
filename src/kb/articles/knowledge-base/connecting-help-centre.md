# Connecting your help centre

If you have an existing help centre (Intercom, Zendesk, Notion, GitBook, or any public URL), you can link it so InboxIQ indexes the content automatically.

## Supported sources

- **Public URLs** — any page with readable text content (Google Docs, Notion, GitBook, Confluence, etc.)
- **Uploaded files** — Markdown, TXT, HTML, PDF (see [Adding articles and past replies](/kb/knowledge-base/adding-content))

## Adding a URL source

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations → Knowledge Base → Manage Knowledge Base**.
2. Under **Add online resource**, paste the URL of your help centre article or documentation page.
3. Click **Add**. InboxIQ fetches and indexes the content.

> **Tip:** Add individual article URLs rather than the root URL of your help centre — InboxIQ indexes one page at a time, not entire sites.

## Keeping content current

InboxIQ does not automatically re-fetch URLs. If your documentation changes, remove the old URL and add the updated one.
