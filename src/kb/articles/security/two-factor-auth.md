# Two-factor authentication (TOTP)

Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds a second layer of security to your InboxIQ account using a time-based one-time password (TOTP) app like Google Authenticator, Authy, or 1Password.

## Enable 2FA

1. Go to **Settings → Security**.
2. Under **Two-factor authentication**, click **Set up 2FA**.
3. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app.
4. Enter the 6-digit code shown in your app to confirm setup.
5. 2FA is now active. You'll be prompted for a code on every sign-in.

## Signing in with 2FA

After entering your email and password, you'll be prompted to enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app. The code refreshes every 30 seconds.

## Supported authenticator apps

- Google Authenticator
- Authy
- 1Password
- Microsoft Authenticator
- Any TOTP-compatible app

## What if I lose access to my authenticator app?

Contact support at [support@kalevent.com](mailto:support@kalevent.com) with verification of your identity. We'll help you regain access and reset 2FA.
