# Team roles and permissions

InboxIQ has two roles: **Owner** and **Member**. The owner is the account creator; additional users are members.

## Role comparison

| Action | Owner | Member |
|---|---|---|
| Connect / disconnect inboxes | ✅ | ❌ |
| Invite teammate inboxes | ✅ | ❌ |
| Configure automation rules | ✅ | ❌ |
| Manage knowledge base | ✅ | ❌ |
| View dashboard and tickets | ✅ | ✅ |
| Reply to emails (via drafts) | ✅ | ✅ |
| Change billing and subscription | ✅ | ❌ |
| Delete the account | ✅ | ❌ |

## Adding team members

Go to **Settings → Team → Invite a teammate**. Enter their email address to send an invitation. They'll create their own password and sign in to your shared workspace.

> Members see the same inbox dashboard as the owner but cannot change settings or integrations.

## Inbox connections and team members

Teammate **inbox connections** (invited via the one-click invite link) are separate from team **members**. A colleague whose inbox is connected does not need an InboxIQ login — their inbox is processed as part of your account but they have no access to the InboxIQ interface.
