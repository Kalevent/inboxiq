# What happens after you connect

Once your inbox is connected, InboxIQ begins working immediately. Here's what to expect in the first few minutes and days.

## First few minutes

InboxIQ polls your inbox and starts processing emails. For each email:

1. A label (Gmail) or category tag (Outlook) is applied.
2. If a draft reply is appropriate, one is created in the thread.
3. Any matching automation rules fire.

You don't need to refresh or do anything — labels will appear in your inbox as emails are processed.

## First few days

InboxIQ's draft quality improves quickly once you:

- **Upload your knowledge base** — go to Settings → Integrations → Knowledge Base and add your help articles, product FAQs, or past reply templates. The AI uses these to ground its drafts.
- **Correct classifications** — if InboxIQ puts an email in the wrong category, move it to the correct InboxIQ label in Gmail (or change the Outlook category). InboxIQ detects the correction and learns.

## What if no emails are being labelled?

If you've connected your inbox but no labels are appearing after 10–15 minutes:

1. Check that your Gmail or Outlook connection shows **Connected** in Settings → Integrations.
2. Confirm that new emails have arrived in your inbox since connecting (InboxIQ only processes new incoming email, not historical emails).
3. See [Email not appearing in InboxIQ](/kb/troubleshooting/email-not-appearing) for further steps.
