# Connect your Gmail inbox

Connecting Gmail takes about two minutes. InboxIQ will request read and modify access so it can apply labels and place draft replies in your threads.

## Steps

1. Sign in to InboxIQ at [kalevent.com](https://kalevent.com).
2. Go to **Settings → Integrations**.
3. Under **Connect your own inbox**, click **Connect Gmail**.
4. A Google sign-in window opens. Choose the Google account whose inbox you want to connect.
5. Review the permissions — InboxIQ requests access to read and modify Gmail (to apply labels and create drafts). Click **Allow**.
6. You'll be redirected to your Gmail inbox. Within a few minutes, InboxIQ will begin processing incoming emails and applying labels.

## What permissions does InboxIQ request?

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Read email messages | To classify and draft replies |
| Modify labels | To apply InboxIQ/Support, InboxIQ/Billing etc. |
| Create drafts | To place draft replies in threads |

InboxIQ does **not** request permission to send email on your behalf. All sends require your action.

## Reconnecting a disconnected inbox

If your Gmail connection expires (Google OAuth tokens expire periodically), you'll see a disconnected status in Settings → Integrations. Click **Reconnect** and follow the same steps above.
