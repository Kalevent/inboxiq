# Connect your Outlook inbox

InboxIQ supports Outlook and Microsoft 365 inboxes via Microsoft OAuth. The process takes about two minutes.

## Steps

1. Sign in to InboxIQ at [kalevent.com](https://kalevent.com).
2. Go to **Settings → Integrations**.
3. Under **Connect your own inbox**, click **Connect Outlook**.
4. A Microsoft sign-in window opens. Choose the Microsoft account whose inbox you want to connect.
5. Review the permissions and click **Accept**.
6. You'll be redirected to your Outlook inbox. InboxIQ will begin classifying incoming emails within minutes.

## How labels appear in Outlook

Outlook doesn't have labels — it uses **categories** instead. InboxIQ creates a set of colour-coded categories in your Outlook account:

| Category | Colour |
|---|---|
| InboxIQ/Support | Blue |
| InboxIQ/Support/Urgent | Red |
| InboxIQ/Billing | Dark red |
| InboxIQ/Updates | Orange |
| InboxIQ/Promotions | Purple |
| InboxIQ/Transactions | Green |
| InboxIQ/Social | Teal |
| InboxIQ/Forums | Cranberry |

These appear as coloured chips on each email row in your inbox.

> **Note:** Unlike Gmail, Outlook categories don't appear as a collapsible group in the sidebar. To filter by category, use Outlook's search and filter by category name.

## Draft replies in Outlook

InboxIQ creates draft replies in your Outlook Drafts folder. The draft is linked to the original thread.
