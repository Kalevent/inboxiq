# Adjusting your confidence threshold

InboxIQ assigns a confidence score to each classification and draft. The confidence threshold controls when InboxIQ acts automatically versus flagging for your review.

## Where to find it

Go to **Settings → AI Features** → Confidence threshold.

## What it controls

- **High threshold (0.85–1.0)** — InboxIQ only acts automatically when it's very confident. More emails will be flagged for review. Recommended when you're just getting started or in a sensitive support environment.
- **Low threshold (0.5–0.75)** — InboxIQ acts on more emails automatically. Suitable when you've trained InboxIQ with a good knowledge base and have seen consistent accuracy.

## Default threshold

The default is **0.75**. This is a conservative starting point that works well for most accounts before significant training data has accumulated.

## When to lower the threshold

Lower the threshold once you've:

1. Uploaded at least 10 knowledge base articles.
2. Operated InboxIQ for 2–4 weeks with regular label corrections.
3. Reviewed the classification accuracy in your dashboard and found it consistently accurate.
