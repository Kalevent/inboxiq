# What happens to my data if I delete my account?

When you delete your InboxIQ account, your data is permanently removed from our systems. This cannot be undone.

## What is deleted

| Data | Timing |
|------|--------|
| Connected inbox OAuth tokens | Immediately — access to your inboxes is revoked |
| Triage configuration and labels | Immediately |
| Automation rules | Immediately |
| AI draft history | Immediately |
| Knowledge base content and embeddings | Immediately |
| Team invitations and sub-user accounts | Immediately |
| Account profile and settings | Immediately |

## What is retained

| Data | Retention period |
|------|-----------------|
| Billing records and invoices | 7 years (legal/tax compliance) |
| Anonymised aggregate usage statistics | Indefinitely (no personal identifiers) |

## What stays in Gmail and Outlook

InboxIQ does not delete anything from your actual Gmail or Outlook account when you delete your InboxIQ account. This means:

- **Labels** created by InboxIQ (e.g. `InboxIQ/Support`) remain in Gmail
- **Categories** created by InboxIQ remain in Outlook
- **Drafts** saved by InboxIQ remain in your Drafts folder

You can delete these manually from within Gmail or Outlook if you wish.

## Cancelling vs. deleting

Cancelling your subscription keeps your account and data intact — InboxIQ just stops processing new emails after your paid period ends. Deleting your account is permanent and removes all data.

For subscription cancellation, see [Managing your subscription](/kb/billing/manage-subscription).
