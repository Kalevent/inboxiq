# I was charged but didn't use InboxIQ this month

InboxIQ subscriptions are billed in advance for the upcoming period, not based on usage. A charge on your statement means your subscription renewed for another month (or year).

## Why you were charged

- Your subscription renewed automatically at the start of a new billing period
- You upgraded your plan mid-period and were charged a prorated amount for the remainder
- A previously failed payment was retried and succeeded

## Check your billing history

1. Go to **Settings → Billing**
2. Click **View invoices** to see a full list of charges with dates and amounts

Each invoice includes the plan, period, and amount charged.

## If the charge looks unexpected

**Charge after cancelling:** If you cancelled your subscription but were still charged, check the cancellation date on your invoice. Cancellations take effect at the end of the paid period — if your card was charged before the cancellation took effect, the charge is for the period that was already active.

**Unknown card charge:** If you don't recognise the charge at all, [contact support](mailto:support@kalevent.com) with the last 4 digits of the card and the charge amount. We will investigate and respond within one business day.

## Requesting a refund

InboxIQ does not offer refunds for unused time in a subscription period. If you believe you were charged in error, contact support within 30 days and we will review the case.

## Cancel to avoid future charges

To stop future renewals, go to **Settings → Billing → Cancel subscription**. Your access continues until the end of the period you've already paid for.
