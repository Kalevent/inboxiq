# Can InboxIQ read all my emails, including personal ones?

InboxIQ reads emails that arrive in your connected inbox's **primary Inbox folder**. It does not distinguish between personal and work emails — if an email lands in your Inbox, InboxIQ will see it.

## What InboxIQ reads

- Emails arriving in your **Inbox** folder after connection
- Subject line, sender, recipient, and body text
- Thread history when composing draft replies

## What InboxIQ does NOT read

- Emails in **Sent**, **Drafts**, **Spam**, or custom folders (unless they pass through Inbox)
- Emails that arrived **before** you connected the inbox
- Attachments are read for classification context but not stored
- Contacts, calendar events, or anything outside of email

## If you'd rather keep personal emails separate

The cleanest approach is to connect only a dedicated work inbox rather than a personal Gmail or Outlook account. InboxIQ works per inbox connection — if you only connect your work address, your personal inbox is never touched.

## Data handling

Email content is processed to generate classifications and draft replies. InboxIQ does not sell your email data or use it to train models for other customers. See our [Privacy Policy](https://kalevent.com/privacy) for full details.
