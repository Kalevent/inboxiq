# Plans and pricing

InboxIQ is priced per inbox connection, not per seat. Every connected inbox — yours or a teammate's — counts as one connection.

## Current plans

Visit [kalevent.com/#pricing](https://kalevent.com/#pricing) for current pricing. Plans are billed monthly or annually; annual billing includes a discount.

## What counts as an inbox connection?

Each Gmail or Outlook inbox you connect counts as one connection. Connecting your own inbox and inviting two teammates' inboxes = 3 connections.

## Free trial

New accounts receive a 14-day free trial with no credit card required. Full functionality is available during the trial.

## Changing your plan

Go to **Settings → Billing** to upgrade, downgrade, or view your current usage.
