One core feature:
Automatic Email → Ticket Conversion

InboxIQ reads every incoming email, understands what the customer wants, and instantly converts it into a structured support ticket. No manual triage, no messy inboxes.

You define simple rules once; InboxIQ handles categorisation, priority, sentiment, and routing in real time.

  • Detects topic: billing, bugs, onboarding, refunds, sales, and more.
  • Extracts key details like order IDs, account numbers, and contact data.
  • Prioritises urgent and high-risk tickets based on sentiment.
  • Automatically routes tickets to the right team or owner.

How it works in 3 steps

  1. 1. Connect your support inbox (Gmail, Outlook, or custom domain) in a few clicks.
  2. 2. InboxIQ learns from your past tickets and emails to build smart routing rules.
  3. 3. New emails are automatically classified and turned into tickets—24/7.

Three big benefits for your support team

1. 80% faster response time

Remove manual triage from your workflow. Your agents start their day with a clean, prioritised queue of tickets ready to answer.

2. Zero missed messages

Every incoming email becomes a trackable ticket. SLAs are easier to meet, and nothing gets buried in a shared inbox ever again.

3. Scale without hiring

As your email volume grows, InboxIQ scales with you. You add customers, not headcount. Support leaders love the predictable cost.
