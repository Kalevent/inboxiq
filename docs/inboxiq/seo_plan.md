# InboxIQ — 90-Day SEO Content Plan

**Goal:**  
Build organic traffic, topical authority, and high-intent signups for InboxIQ  
(primary focus: AI support agent, AI email triage, ticket automation)

**Audience:**  
Small support teams, startups, SMBs, founders, support leads

**Publishing cadence:**  
2 articles per week (~24–26 posts in 90 days)

---

## Core SEO Strategy

**Primary topic cluster**
- AI support agent
- AI email triage
- Email → ticket automation

**Secondary clusters**
- Support inbox management
- AI helpdesk alternatives
- Customer support efficiency
- Sentiment analysis & prioritisation

## Measurement & KPIs

Track weekly:

- Google Search Console
  - Impressions
  - Indexed pages
  - Clicks
- Keyword movement for:
  - AI support agent
  - AI email triage
  - Zendesk AI alternative
- Blog → signup conversions
- Time on page (>2 minutes target)
- Stage metrics (map to admin “Blog / SEO” card)
  - TOFU: impressions, time on page
  - MOFU: internal clicks (to MOFU/BOFU pages), demo views
  - BOFU: trial signups
  - Trial: activation (inbox connected + ticket created)
- Planned data sources
  - GSC for impressions, clicks, indexed pages, rankings
  - Analytics (GA4) for time on page, internal clicks, demo views, signups
  - Attribution for blog → signup (landing page /blog/*)

## Publishing Checklist (Per Article)

- One primary keyword per page  
- Keyword in:
  - H1
  - Meta title
  - First 100 words
  - At least one H2
- Internal links to:
  - Homepage
  - Pricing page
  - Feature pages
- CTA after ~60–70% scroll
- Clear problem → solution narrative
