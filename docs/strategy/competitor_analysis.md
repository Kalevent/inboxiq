## Competitors to analyse (for B2B SaaS support email ICP)

When ready, analyse these:

### Direct (AI-powered support automation)
- Intercom (Fin AI)
- Zendesk (AI features)
- Freshdesk (Freddy AI)
- Help Scout (AI Assist)
- Front, 
- Superhuman

### Adjacent (workflow automation)
- Microsoft Power Automate — enterprise workflow tool, not AI-first, complex setup, targets large IT-managed organisations. Different buyer (IT admin vs founder). Not a direct threat at SMB level but worth understanding because some buyers will compare.
- Google Workspace Studio (Gemini for Workspace) — very new, primarily targets existing Google Workspace enterprise users. Not self-serve for SMBs. Monitor but low priority.

### Point tools (cheaper, narrower)
- Tidio — e-commerce chat/AI, not for B2B SaaS support email
- Gorgias — Shopify-native support, e-commerce focus
- Forethought — AI for enterprise support teams, expensive

## Key question to answer for each competitor

1. Who is their actual buyer? (IT admin vs founder vs CS manager)
2. What does their self-serve setup look like?
3. What is their lowest pricing tier?
4. What does their AI actually do vs promise?
5. Where are customers complaining about them? (G2, Trustpilot, Reddit)

Finding where established competitors are failing their SMB customers is where InboxIQ wins.
