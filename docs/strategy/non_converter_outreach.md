# Re-engagement Email — Non-converters & Trialists

## Target audience
- 57 people who opened the original outreach email but did not sign up
- Any trialists who signed up but went inactive

## Subject line options (pick one)
- You opened my email but didn't sign up — that's useful
- Did I build the wrong thing?
- One honest question about your inbox

---

## Email body

---

Hi [First name],

A few weeks ago I sent you an email with the subject line "Before you hire a support manager."

You opened it. But you didn't sign up.

That gap is more useful to me than a conversion. It means something wasn't right — the problem, the solution, the timing, or the price — and I'd rather know which one than guess.

I'm not going to pitch you again. I'm asking for 90 seconds of honest feedback.

Three questions: https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfJK4iqU9jJC-9inutItDspoxrqWMXgCDAIrWZyPgQE906ZqA/viewform

That's it. No follow-up sales call unless you want one.

Kofi
Founder, InboxIQ

---

## Notes on tone
- No product description — they already saw it
- No urgency or scarcity language
- "I'd rather know than guess" signals genuine curiosity, not desperation
- Offering to skip the sales call removes the most common friction for clicking a feedback link

---

## For trialists (slight variation)

Same subject options. Change the second paragraph to:

> You signed up for a trial but I haven't heard from you since. That tells me something wasn't working — either the product, the onboarding, or the timing. I'd rather find out than assume.
