## Purpose

This document records completion of annual security awareness training by all Kalevent Ltd team members with access to InboxIQ production systems. Maps to SOC2 CC1.4 (security awareness training).

---

## Training Programme

**Format:** Self-paced quiz covering core security topics  
**Frequency:** Annually (and on onboarding for new team members)  
**Pass mark:** 80%  
**Topics covered:**
- Phishing recognition and reporting
- Password hygiene and use of a password manager
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) — why and how
- Handling customer data — need-to-know principle
- Incident reporting — how to escalate a suspected breach
- Acceptable use of company systems and credentials
- Secure coding basics (SQL injection, XSS, secrets in code)

## Training Questions (Google Form)

The quiz is administered via Google Forms. A copy of the form and timestamped response export is stored in the InboxIQ Security folder on Google Drive (Shared Drive — not personal).

**Sample questions used:**
1. You receive an email from "support@g00gle.com" asking you to reset your password. What do you do?
2. Where should API keys and database passwords be stored?
3. A customer emails asking you to send them a copy of another customer's data "for their records." What do you do?
4. You notice an unusual login from a foreign IP on the AWS console. What is the first action to take?
5. What is the minimum scope principle when granting IAM access?

---

## New Employee Onboarding

Any new team member with access to production systems must complete the security awareness quiz within their first 5 working days. Access to production credentials is not granted until training is confirmed complete.
